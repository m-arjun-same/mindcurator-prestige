Questions

How much does a website cost?

Websites start at ₹10,000. Bigger work is quoted in writing before anything starts — no price is ever a surprise.

How long does it take?

A landing page takes days; a full site takes longer. Your timeline is told upfront, in the agreement.

What if I don't like the design?

Then you don't pay. The sample comes first — you only pay if you love it.

How many changes can I make?

Two preview revision rounds are included. Further changes are priced in writing first, so you always decide before anything is charged.

When do I pay?

Nothing upfront for the sample. The invoice is due on receipt, before go-live.

Do you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Send us the link — we keep what works and rebuild the rest.

What do I need to give you?

Your business details, a logo and photos if you have them, and what the site must do. We guide you through the rest.

What do I get at the end?

A live site, plus the files, access and the domain in your name. You own your site.

Can you add forms, WhatsApp or Instagram?

Yes — an enquiry form that lands in your email, a one-tap WhatsApp button, Instagram, Google Maps and call buttons.

How do we start?

WhatsApp, an Instagram DM, the form below, or email. Tell us about your business — we reply with the next step.
